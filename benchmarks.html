Key Observations

  • Optimal parallelism = 4 workers, matching the M3's 4 performance cores.
  • Super-linear speedup at 2 workers for prove (5.1×): single-threaded execution is memory-latency-bound (47% CPU utilization); a second thread overlaps memory waits.
  • Degradation at 8 workers: engaging efficiency cores + thermal throttling on the fanless MacBook Air reduces throughput. System time doubles from 13.3s (p=4) to 32.0s (p=8).
  • Verify is less parallelizable than prove: max speedup 2.2× vs 6.8×. Verify has a higher serial fraction.
